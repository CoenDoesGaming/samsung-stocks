# One Stocks Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

One Stocks is a stock, ETF and currency tracker for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

One Stocks does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

## Information stored on your device

Your watchlist, display preferences, recent searches, and cached prices and news are saved only on your device. They are never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes them.

## Information sent over the internet

To show prices, charts, search results and news, the app requests market data directly from Yahoo Finance. These requests include the ticker symbols in your watchlist and any text you type into search. Like any internet request, they also reveal your device's IP address to Yahoo. The developer does not receive or see these requests. Yahoo's handling of them is covered by the [Yahoo Privacy Policy](https://legal.yahoo.com/us/en/yahoo/privacy/index.html).

## Voice search

If you tap the microphone in search, the app uses the speech recognition service already on your device (for example, Google's). The app receives only the recognised text and does not record or store audio. The app does not request microphone permission itself.

## News links

Tapping a news story opens it in your web browser. The site you visit has its own privacy policy.

## Permissions

The app uses internet access to fetch market data, and background and start-up permissions to keep its widgets up to date. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or advertising ID.

## Children

One Stocks is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
